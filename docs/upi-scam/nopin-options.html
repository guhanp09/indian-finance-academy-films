Negating the PIN Pad

The RECEIVE side has to say no PIN is involved in receiving money. Right now a thin red line is laid across the keypad. Five alternatives, animated on one clock so the motion is what you compare. Nothing here is in the film yet.

Why the line is weak

  • It is an annotation, not an event. Everything else in this film arrives with mass, lands, is flung, is absorbed. The line simply appears.
  • It leaves its target unchanged. The keypad underneath keeps its colour and brightness. It still looks available.
  • It clips only part of the grid. Two keys are never touched. Partial coverage reads as some, not none.
  • The pad is not identified. On this side it is drawn without digits — a grid of blank blobs. SEND has its numbers.
  • The red is borrowed. Red means money leaving the account. Here it is asked to mean forbidden, in the one frame the colour script calls the cleanest in the film.
