## SENTENCE 1
The scammer will send you a UPI request that looks like money is coming to you — you just need to approve it by entering your UPI PIN.

## SENTENCE 2
The moment you enter your PIN and approve it, however, instead of you getting paid, the money leaves your account.

## SENTENCE 3
While the common targets for this scam are online sellers, anyone unfamiliar with the basics of UPI transactions can fall for it.

## SENTENCE 4
If your parents use UPI, let them know that they never need to enter their UPI pin to "receive" money, only to send it.
